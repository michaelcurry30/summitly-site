Support

Something not working, or an idea to share? Email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. Replies usually come within two days.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes your data, so there is no cloud copy to restore from.

My timer ended while the app was closed. Allow notifications for Summitly in Settings and you will get an alert when a climb ends. Open the app to bank your meters.

What is strict mode? It adds 25% to your meters, but leaving the app for more than 20 seconds drops the climb.

Subscription

How do I cancel? Open Summitly, go to the Climb tab, and tap Manage or cancel. You can also open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Summitly Pro. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and you will not be charged again. The lifetime plan is a one-time purchase and has nothing to cancel.

I subscribed on another phone, or reinstalled. Tap Restore purchases on the subscribe screen or in the Climb tab. Use the same Apple ID.

I want a refund. Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the Summitly Pro purchase. Refunds follow Apple's policy.
