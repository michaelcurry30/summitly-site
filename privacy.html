Summitly Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Summitly is a focus timer with tasks, habits, notes and rewards. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your information stays on your phone, and we do not collect it.

What the app stores

Everything you enter, including tasks, habits, notes, rewards, focus sessions, streaks and settings, is saved only on your device. The developer cannot see it and it is not uploaded to any server.

What we collect

Nothing from the app itself. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party software that collects data. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Subscriptions and payments

Summitly is unlocked with Summitly Pro, which you can buy as an auto-renewing monthly subscription, an auto-renewing yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Monthly and yearly plans may start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, and then renew at the listed price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see or store your card details, name, email or Apple ID. The app asks Apple on your device whether your subscription is active, and nothing else is sent to us. You can cancel any time in Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device, such as one when a focus session ends or a daily reminder at a time you pick. These are created and delivered by your phone. No notification content is sent to us.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information, because we do not have it.

Deleting your data

Use Start over in the Climb tab, or delete the app. Either one removes everything the app saved on your device. If you back up your phone with iCloud or a computer, copies in your own backup are controlled by you and Apple.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
