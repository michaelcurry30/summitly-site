## Story (one idea)
Problem: the phone pulls him back with a rope every time it buzzes → struggle: tug-of-war with the Scroll Goblin, he fights it and loses → turn: "Not anymore." He slices NOTIFICATIONS, carries the phone to another room, shuts it in a box, and walking back the rope stretches and SNAPS → payoff: focus is easy; on the mountain he cuts off the last stub of rope. Lesson line: "DON'T FIGHT TEMPTATION. / REMOVE IT." Sign-off: "FOLLOW FOR MORE".

## Assumptions
- The rope is a visual metaphor for the pull of a phone in reach (no numbers, no study statistics on screen).
- Payoff + sign-off = 5 + 3 = 8 words on screen.
