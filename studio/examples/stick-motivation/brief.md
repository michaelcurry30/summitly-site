## Story (one idea)
Problem: he waits for motivation (a sparkly cloud) that teases and floats away → struggle: he waits for "the perfect mood", clock spinning → turn: his excuse WHEN I FEEL LIKE IT slams in and he slices it, then starts before he feels ready → payoff: as he works, a MOTIVATION meter rises and the cloud finally catches up and rains sparkles on him; it then follows him like a pet. Lesson line: "ACTION FIRST. / MOTIVATION FOLLOWS." Sign-off: "FOLLOW FOR MORE".

## Assumptions
- The MOTIVATION meter is the kit's gauge with a new label; it shows no numbers or scale.
- Payoff + sign-off = 4 + 3 = 7 words on screen.
