# Brief — stick-habit-stack ("HABIT STACKING")
- **Who watches:** short-video viewers; app channel and ebook channel.
- **Where it plays:** phone, vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps.
- **How long:** 27.0 s neutral; 29.9 s app (+2.3 s beat with a real Summitly habits screenshot + 3.3 s end card).
- **Lead with:** "New habits don't stick" — the ninja slaps a STRETCH sticky note on the wall and it falls off, twice.
- **Use my files:** no statistics; "1 page" and "1 minute" are example habits.
## Story
"I'll remember" (you won't) → "Not anymore." WORD CUT on I'LL REMEMBER → the fix: habit stacking, blocks snap together → chain the new habit to an old one → after coffee: read 1 page (kitchen) → after brushing teeth: 1 minute of stretching (bathroom) → the old habit becomes the trigger (the tower lights up from the bottom) → lesson "After I ___, I will ___."
